About Me

I am a PhD researcher at the University of Birmingham specialising in hydrological modelling, streamflow prediction, and physics-informed machine learning. My work focuses on developing hybrid modelling frameworks that bridge process-based hydrology with modern data-driven approaches to improve predictive performance across diverse catchments in the UK.

I am passionate about addressing real-world challenges in water systems and climate change through innovative, interpretable, and robust computational models.

Research & Experience

Doctoral Researcher

University of Birmingham, United Kingdom — Oct 2023 – Present

  • Developing and applying a Physics-Informed Machine Learning (PIML) framework for streamflow and reservoir modelling using the CAMELS-GB dataset, integrating process-based hydrological models with machine learning to improve predictive performance across diverse UK catchments.
  • Designing a signature-enhanced hybrid modelling framework to better represent complex hydrological processes and catchment variability, improving model interpretability and robustness.
  • Investigating hydrological signatures (Baseflow Index, Flashiness Index, Rainfall–Runoff Ratio, seasonality) to enhance predictions in regulated and heterogeneous catchments.
  • Leading research titled “Bridging Physics and Machine Learning: A Signature-Enhanced Hybrid Framework for Streamflow Prediction in Complex Catchments” — selected for an oral presentation at EGU 2025 (European Geosciences Union).
